Frequently asked questions

What do I have to provide?

Photos of your work, keywords about your services and two approvals: one for the concept, one for the finished site. I take care of the rest.

Who writes the texts?

If you have no time to write, I write them for €60–90 per subpage. You approve them.

What does it cost per month?

Nothing with the One-pager, where care is optional. With the Business website, care from €60/month is included, for at least 3 months, then cancellable monthly.

Do I own the domain?

Yes. The domain is always registered in your name, never mine. You get all access details.

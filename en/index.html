    Services and pricing in detail

    How a project works.

    1. Intro call

      30 minutes, free. You tell me what your business does and what the site should achieve.

    2. Concept for approval

      You see structure, copy and design before I build. Nothing moves on without your OK.

    3. Build

      I build, you see work in progress. 2 to 4 rounds of revisions are included, depending on the package.

    4. Handover

      The site goes live. You get all access details and a short guide.

    Payment for One-pager and Business website: 50 % on order, 50 % on completion. For Premium and Shop in instalments: 40 % at start, 30 % after concept approval, 30 % on completion.

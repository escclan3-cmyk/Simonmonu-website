An invented coaching practice in Vienna-Neubau (the demo is in German). A one-pager with three offers, prices including VAT and a single button.

Open in a new tab ↗

Starting point

Coaching lives on people trusting the person before they know them. The site has to be calm, say what it costs and offer a single next step.

Decision

A warm light ground, deep green for seriousness and an orange as the only warmth. Three offers with prices including VAT, one button to get to know each other.

Build

  • Ground #F5F4F0 13.0 : 1 against #1A2E26
    Page ground, text in deep green
  • Deep green #1A2E26 13.0 : 1 against #F5F4F0
    Surfaces and text
  • Orange #E8914A 5.9 : 1 against #1A2E26
    Accent, only for the button and emphasis
TypefacesSpectral and Hanken Grotesk, self-hosted.
StructureA one-pager with several sections.
LabellingIn the footer of the demo and next to the client quotes it says that person and quotes are invented.

Limits

It is a demo. Person, offers and prices are invented. On a real job it would be your voice, your photos and your prices, and I would ask you for real client quotes instead of inventing any.

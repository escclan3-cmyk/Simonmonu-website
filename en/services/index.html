What is not included.

To keep a fixed price fair, every quote also says what is not part of it.

  • Unlimited revision rounds. The quote states how many rounds are included. Further rounds are billed separately.
  • Copy, integrations and shop features unless agreed. Whatever is needed goes into the quote as its own item.
  • Third-party costs. Hosting, licences, stock photos, translation and ongoing SEO work are listed separately.
  • Delays with content. You deliver content and feedback on the agreed dates. If something comes later, the launch moves.

Process and payment

  1. Intro call

    About 30 minutes, free. We talk about the occasion, goal, timeframe and budget. If I am not the right person, I will tell you.

  2. Quote and contract

    You get a fixed price and an exact description of what is included. The project starts with the deposit.

  3. Workshop and structure

    Together we work out who the site should reach and what it needs to say. The page structure and copy come out of that.

  4. Design for approval

    You see the design before I code it, and you approve it.

  5. Build and testing

    I code the site and test it on real devices, from links and forms to load time and accessibility.

  6. Launch and handover

    After your sign-off the site goes live. You get all access details.

Website projects: 40 % at start, 30 % after design approval, 30 % before handover. Website check, motion design, graphic design and add-ons: 50 % on order, 50 % on delivery. Care is billed monthly in advance. Scope, your input, revision rounds, sign-off and payment plan are set out in writing in the quote.

Frequently asked questions

Why a range instead of a fixed price?

The range shows roughly where your project sits. After the intro call I give you a fixed price, and it holds even if the work takes me longer than planned.

Do you make free drafts?

No. In the intro call I tell you openly what I notice about your current site. A worked-out concept is part of a project. If you only want an assessment first, start with the website check.

How long does it take?

We set the schedule in the quote. It depends most on how quickly your copy, images and feedback arrive.

Why no WordPress?

I code the sites myself. That means no database and no plug-ins that go out of date or get attacked, so maintenance stays small. If you really need WordPress, I will tell you honestly whether I am the right person for it.

Do I own the domain?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, and you get all access details.

Do I need Care?

Not necessarily. If your site rarely changes, I will tell you. Care is worth it when new content, landing pages or improvements come up regularly.

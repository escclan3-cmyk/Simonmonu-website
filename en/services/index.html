What is not included.

A fixed price only works with clear limits. They are in every quote.

  • Unlimited revision rounds. The quote states how many rounds are included. Further rounds are billed separately.
  • Copy, integrations and shop features without agreement. Whatever is needed goes into the quote as its own item.
  • Third-party costs. Hosting, licences, stock photos, translation and ongoing SEO work are listed separately.
  • Waiting for content. You deliver content and feedback on the agreed dates. If something comes later, the launch moves.

Process and payment

  1. Intro call

    30 minutes, free. Occasion, goal, timeframe and budget. If it is not a fit, I will say so openly.

  2. Quote and contract

    A fixed price with a written scope. The project starts with the deposit.

  3. Workshop and structure

    We clarify audience, offer and message. The sitemap and content come out of that.

  4. Design for approval

    You see the design before I code. Nothing moves on without your OK.

  5. Build and testing

    I build in code and test on real devices: links, forms, load time, accessibility.

  6. Launch and handover

    After your sign-off the site goes live. You get all access details.

Website projects: 40 % at start, 30 % after design approval, 30 % before handover. Website check, motion design, graphic design and add-ons: 50 % on order, 50 % on delivery. Care is billed monthly in advance. Scope, your input, revision rounds, sign-off and payment plan are set out in writing in the quote.

Frequently asked questions

Why a range instead of a fixed price?

The range shows roughly where your project sits. After the intro call you get a fixed price with a written scope. That price then applies, even if I need longer.

Do you make free drafts?

No. In the intro call I tell you honestly what stands out about your site. A full concept is part of a project, or you start with the website check.

How long does it take?

We set the schedule in the quote. It depends mostly on when content and feedback arrive.

Why no WordPress?

I build in code. There is a practical reason: there is no database and no plug-ins that can go out of date and be attacked. That is why care stays small. If you explicitly need WordPress, I will tell you openly.

Do I own the domain?

Yes. The domain is always registered in your name, never mine. You get all access details.

Do I need Care?

Not every website needs ongoing care. If little changes on your side, I will tell you. Care pays off when content, landing pages or improvements come up regularly.

Is this right for your project?

An intro call costs you 30 minutes. Free and non-binding.

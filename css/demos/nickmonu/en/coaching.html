Someone who still rehearses teaches differently.

I have been on stage since 1989, in the German-speaking theatre since 1994, and I direct. What I pass on in class comes from productions I am working on, not from a textbook.

My path adds something particular: I trained in England and I perform in both languages. When a role has to be played in English, we work on thinking in English rather than translating. Speaking a language fluently and acting in it are two different things.

Born in Lagos, schooled in England, studied in Washington, trained as an actor in London at the Webber Douglas Academy of Dramatic Art. Then the Moscow Art Theatre, the Schaubühne Berlin from 1994 and the Burgtheater Vienna from 2003.

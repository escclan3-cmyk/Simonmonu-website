Privacy

In short: this website sets no cookies, counts no visitors and loads nothing from third parties until you click on it yourself.

This is a translation. The German version is legally binding.

Controller

Nicholas Monu, Eichetstraße 51, 5071 Wals-Siezenheim, Austria. For any privacy question, an email to nick@nickmonu.com is enough.

When you visit the website

The website is hosted by a web hosting provider that delivers it on my behalf (data processing under Art. 28 GDPR). The server processes technically necessary access data: IP address, date and time, the page requested, browser and operating system. This is needed to deliver the page and to fend off attacks. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Logs are kept only as long as these purposes require.

No cookies, no statistics

There are no cookies, no visitor counting, no advertising or analytics services.

A single item is stored in your browser: a flag in session storage (sessionStorage, name “nm-eintritt”, value “1”). It makes sure the short opening screen with the name runs only once per visit rather than on every page. The flag never leaves your device, is not evaluated and disappears when you close the tab or browser. As it only serves to display the website you requested as intended, it is permitted without consent under § 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021.

Typefaces

The typefaces are stored on the same server as the website. There is no connection to Google Fonts or any other font service.

Showreel

The showreel is hosted by Filmmakers Europe (filmmakers.one GmbH, Gocher Straße 19, 50733 Cologne, Germany), and it cannot technically be embedded on this website. The “Watch” button is therefore an ordinary link: it opens the video page on filmmakers.eu in a new tab, just like the IMDb or Filmmakers links in the footer. As long as you do not click it, no connection to filmmakers.eu is made and no data is transferred. Once you click, you leave this website, and the Filmmakers privacy policy applies: filmmakers.eu/en/privacy.

Enquiry form

When you send the form, your details are processed: name, email address, optionally a phone number, and the details of your project. I use them only to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is steps prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). I delete the data once the enquiry is dealt with, unless statutory retention periods apply.

The form is delivered via Formspree (Formspree, Inc., USA), which receives the form data and forwards it to me by email. The transfer to the USA is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). If you prefer not to use it, simply email me directly.

If delivery via Formspree is not available, the form opens your email program with a prepared message instead. The data then goes directly to me through your own email provider.

Email

When you write to me, I process your message and address in order to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once the matter is closed, unless retention obligations apply.

Links to other websites

Links to IMDb, Filmmakers, newspapers and theatres are ordinary links. You only leave this website when you click one; the privacy policy of that provider then applies.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You can withdraw consent at any time. An email to nick@nickmonu.com is enough.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority: Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at.

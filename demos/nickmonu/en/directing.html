Directing

Directing is an extension of acting.

After training in London I went to the Moscow Art Theatre, then to the Schaubühne Berlin under Andrea Breth and to the Burgtheater Vienna. In Nigeria I founded my own theatre company, and in Salzburg the Ensemble Juvavum in 2013. I direct classics and new plays, in German and in English.
